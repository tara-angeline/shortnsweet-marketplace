About

Bringing indulgent dessert boxes to Dublin, one sweet layer at a time.

Short N' Sweet started with a simple idea - desserts should feel exciting, indulgent, and easy to enjoy anywhere. Instead of traditional slices of cake, we wanted to create something playful: beautifully layered desserts served in a box.

Each dessert box is carefully assembled with fresh cakes, silky creams, and bold flavours inspired by both classic European pastries and vibrant Southeast Asian desserts.

Whether you're celebrating something special or simply craving a sweet escape, our dessert boxes are designed to bring a little joy to your day.
